Scatterloom Help

Scatterloom captures audio into a grain pool and weaves those grains into rhythms using a Markov probability matrix.

Get started

Scatterloom requires MPC OS 3.10.1 or later and external plugin support. It does not require a license key or an internet connection to use.

Explore Scatterloom

Use the Main page to capture and play grains, Markov to shape the order of playback, Mix to adjust the mix, and Grains to adjust capture and grain settings.
