Get started

Scatterloom is an effect for standalone MPC. It listens to incoming audio, captures short pieces called grains, and plays them back in a rhythm. It can hold up to nine grains.

  1. Install Scatterloom using the OpenMPC Plugin Manager. Scatterloom requires MPC OS 3.10.1 or later and external plugin support.
  2. Add Scatterloom as an effect on a track or return receiving audio.
  3. Start the MPC transport, enable Capture, and play audio into the effect. The capture indicator flashes when a transient is detected; the grain pads show the occupied pool positions.
  4. Turn Capture off to keep the current pool. Adjust Mix on the Mix page to hear the balance of input and effect.
  5. Try the Main page’s rhythm controls, then explore the Markov page to change the order in which grains are chosen.

Scatterloom does not require a license key or an internet connection to use.

Main: capture and rhythm

Capture and the trigger indicator
Capture enables adding incoming grains to the pool. The slender indicator flashes when the input detector finds a transient, including while Capture is off. Adjust capture sensitivity on the Grains page.
Grain pads
The three-by-three grid represents the grain pool. Occupied pads light up; tap one to audition that grain over the current output. Grain 1 is at the lower left and grain 9 at the upper right.
Clear Pool and undo
Clear Pool removes the captured grains. The circular-arrow button removes the most recently captured grain; it does not restore an older grain displaced from a full pool. The Grains page lets you turn the clear confirmation on or off.
Play / Pause
The playback button starts or pauses Scatterloom’s rhythmic playback. When playback is stopped, incoming audio passes through; the grain pads remain available for auditioning.
Steps and Pulses
Steps sets the length of the Euclidean rhythm. Pulses sets the number of hits within it. For example, five pulses over sixteen steps distributes five hits across a sixteen-step cycle. The ring visualizes the pattern, and its center shows pulses over steps.
Rotate and Division
Rotate shifts the pattern around its cycle. Division sets the rhythmic rate relative to the MPC tempo. Automatic rotation can be selected on the Grains page.
Grain Length, Reverse Chance, and Recent Grains
Grain Length changes the duration of playback. Reverse Chance controls how often grains play backward. Recent Grains limits how many of the most recently captured grains are available to the pattern.

Markov: choose what plays next

The grid describes transitions between grains. Each row is the current grain, and each column is a possible next grain. A cell’s bar shows the chance of that transition; the row’s probabilities add up to 100%.

  1. Tap a cell, or use Current Grain and Next Grain to select it.
  2. Adjust Probability with the control or the MPC encoder. The other probabilities in that row are redistributed to keep the total at 100%.
  3. Use Patterns to load a starting pattern. Choosing a pattern replaces the current matrix; editing a cell makes the matrix custom.

As Played follows the captured order; Reverse goes the other way; Random gives available grains equal weight. Other patterns favor different kinds of movement. Preset patterns adapt as the pool grows or shrinks; custom edits are preserved. Unavailable rows and columns are dimmed.

Mix: level, looping, and stereo

Mix
Balances the incoming signal and Scatterloom’s processed signal.
Loop Feedback and Loop Time
Loop Time sets the loop’s rhythmic length; Loop Feedback controls how much signal recirculates through it.
Grain pan
The pan controls set each occupied grain’s position in the stereo field. Empty pool positions are unavailable. The mode buttons offer Center, Left to Right, Right to Left, and Spread. When an automatic mode is latched, it controls the pan positions; turn the mode off to edit them manually.

Grains: capture and grain settings

Capture Sensitivity and Auto Sens
Sensitivity adjusts transient detection. Auto Sens enables automatic sensitivity. Watch the indicator while adjusting to see when the detector triggers.
Attack
Adjusts the attack of a grain.
Grain Start
Adjusts the grain’s start timing.
Rotation Mode
Choose Manual or an interval in beats for automatic pattern rotation. The Rotate control on Main shows the current rotation.
Confirm Clear
When enabled, Clear Pool asks for confirmation. Turn it off if you want Clear Pool to act immediately.

Saving your work

Scatterloom saves its settings, Markov probabilities, and captured grain pool with the plugin state in your MPC project. Save the project to recall the sound later. A grain still being captured at save time is not included.

Troubleshooting

No grains are appearing
Confirm audio is reaching the effect, Capture is enabled, and the trigger indicator flashes. Adjust Sensitivity or Auto Sens on Grains.
No rhythmic playback
Check that the MPC transport is running, Scatterloom’s playback is enabled, the pool contains grains, and Mix is set so the effect is audible.
A grid cell cannot be selected
Only occupied grain positions are active. Capture more grains to make additional rows and columns available.
A grain pan won’t stay where I put it
Check whether an automatic pan mode is latched on the Mix page. Turn it off to set pans manually.
